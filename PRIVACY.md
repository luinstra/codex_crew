# Privacy Policy

Codex Crew does not collect, transmit, or store personal data outside the local files it creates in your own environment.

The plugin may create project-local workflow state under `.codex-crew/` and may copy custom agent templates into `~/.codex/agents/` when you run the installer. Those files stay on your machine unless you choose to share them.

Codex itself, GitHub, and any external tools you use with this plugin are governed by their own privacy policies.
